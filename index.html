System Architecture

Fraud alerts enter the orchestrator and flow through four specialised agents. Conditional routing escalates high-risk cases immediately, while guardrails and audit logging run at every step.

Conditional Routing Logic

IF composite_risk >= 75 OR critical_policy_violation: → BLOCK & ESCALATE (SLA: 1 hour) ELIF composite_risk >= 50: → HOLD & VERIFY (SLA: 4 hours) ELIF composite_risk >= 25: → FLAG & MONITOR (SLA: 24 hours) ELSE: → APPROVE WITH NOTE

Agent Roles

Each agent is a self-contained analysis module with defined inputs, outputs, and a model-specific explanation generator for transparent decision-making.

📊

Transaction Analysis Agent

Evaluates transaction amount deviation, timing anomalies, channel/device signals, location mismatches, recipient risk category, and rule-trigger severity.

Out → risk_score (0-100), classification, findings[], explanation
👤

Customer Behaviour Agent

Assesses account maturity trust, login anomalies, credential/device changes, prior fraud history, customer vulnerability profile, and behavioural consistency.

Out → anomaly_score, trust_adjusted, anomaly_level, findings[]
📜

Risk & Policy Agent

Applies bank threshold policies, RBI AML/KYC guidelines, PMLA reporting rules, elderly protection protocols, and cross-border fraud procedures.

Out → composite_risk, policy_violations[], regulatory_flags[], hitl_required
🎯

Recommendation Agent

Synthesises all prior outputs into a final decision with confidence score, urgency level, actionable next steps, and hallucination self-check flags.

Out → decision, confidence%, urgency, actions[], evidence_summary

Case 001: Normal — Legitimate Family Transfer

Scenario: Rajesh Mehta, a software engineer in Pune with 8 years of account history, sends ₹48,000 via UPI to his brother at 9:23 AM from his registered device.

  • Amount is 1.4x his average (₹35,000) — mild deviation
  • Known device, known recipient, normal hours
  • 1 low-severity rule triggered (amount > 2x daily avg)
  • Zero failed logins, no credential changes

System Decision: APPROVE — Confidence 95%, Urgency LOW, HITL not required

Case 002: Ambiguous — Velocity Breach + New Device

Scenario: Priya Sharma, a freelance designer in Mumbai, sends ₹95,000 via NEFT to an unregistered beneficiary at 2:47 AM from a new device in Delhi.

  • Amount is 3.8x her average — significant deviation
  • New device, first-time recipient, unusual hours
  • 4 rules triggered (2 High, 2 Medium) including geo-mismatch
  • 2 failed login attempts, recent device change
  • Could be legitimate: freelancer paying a new client while travelling

System Decision: HOLD & VERIFY — Confidence 70%, Urgency HIGH, HITL required

Case 003: High-Risk — Account Takeover (Elderly Customer)

Scenario: Vikram Desai, a 67-year-old retired officer in Bengaluru with 22 years of account history, sends ₹4,90,000 via RTGS to a crypto exchange wallet at 4:12 AM from a VPN in Lagos, Nigeria.

  • Amount is 32.7x his average — extreme deviation
  • Unknown device, VPN from Nigeria, crypto destination
  • 6 rules triggered (4 Critical, 2 High)
  • 7 failed logins followed by password change then immediate high-value transfer
  • Elderly customer — heightened vulnerability to social engineering

System Decision: BLOCK & ESCALATE — Confidence 98%, Urgency CRITICAL, HITL mandatory

Governance & Guardrails

Eight guardrails enforce safety at the input, process, output, and audit layers of the pipeline.

🔒
PII Masking

Customer names, account numbers, and device IDs are masked before model inference to prevent data leakage.

📐
Schema Validation

Every agent input and output is validated against a typed schema. Malformed data is rejected before processing.

👨‍💼
Mandatory HITL

Cases with composite risk > 40 or two or more policy violations require human approval before any action is taken.

⏰
SLA Enforcement

Critical cases have a 1-hour SLA; high-priority cases 4 hours. Breaches trigger automatic escalation.

🔍
Hallucination Detection

The Recommendation Agent cross-checks its claims against upstream evidence and flags unsupported assertions.

📋
Structured Output Check

All agent outputs are verified for JSON compliance, required field presence, and value-range correctness.

📝
Full Audit Trail

Every pipeline step, model call, HITL decision, and override is timestamped and logged for regulatory review.

⚖️
Regulatory Compliance

Automated checks against RBI AML/KYC guidelines, PMLA STR requirements, and FATF Travel Rule obligations.
